When reporting an issue

  • Game: Moonjunk Workshop / 달빛 고물상
  • Test build: App version, device model, and iOS or Android version, if known
  • What happened: What you were doing, what you expected, and what actually happened
  • Optional attachment: A screenshot with personal information and notifications hidden

Do not send passwords, verification codes, sign-in tokens, card details, identity documents, or a full device backup. A description of the issue is enough to begin.

A save or sign-in problem?

Please preserve the current state instead of immediately deleting or reinstalling a test app. A device-only workshop can be separate from an account’s server save. Sign-in availability depends on the test build and service configuration.

For deletion, see the account deletion guide. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not complete server account deletion.

Replies and personal information

Kim Hyeonwoo reviews enquiries. We do not promise a fixed response time. When you email us, your reply address, message, and chosen attachments are used to handle your request. See the privacy notice for details and how to request deletion of correspondence.
