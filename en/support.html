Where to contact us

Development and privacy inquiries: ialskdji@gmail.com. A fixed response time is not promised.

Describing a problem

Include the app version if known, device model and OS version, what happened, and what you expected. Screenshots are optional; hide personal information and notifications.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, login tokens, card details, identity documents, complete saves or device backups, or health records. A description of the issue is enough to start.

Preserve test saves

Please preserve the current state instead of immediately deleting or resetting a test app to troubleshoot. Device saves and account information may have different scopes. Check the instructions for your test version rather than assuming an unavailable feature can be used.

The privacy notice explains email use and deletion requests. The account deletion page explains account-related requests.
