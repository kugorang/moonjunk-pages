Moonjunk Workshop · Your privacy

Privacy notice

This notice covers the current prelaunch information website and email support. It does not replace a final privacy policy for the game app, which is not yet offered to the general public.

Last updated: September 13, 2026

The website and the app in development are separate.You cannot sign in to the game, create a game account, watch ads, or make purchases on this site. Visiting this website does not provide us with your game save or sign-in credentials.

1. Operator and contact

Moonjunk Workshop / 달빛 고물상 is operated by Kim Hyeonwoo (김현우). For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information, email ialskdji@gmail.com.

2. Visiting this website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no visitor analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, browser storage, contact forms, or external fonts. Language links open another language’s page without saving a preference.

GitHub, the hosting provider, states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. The operator cannot directly erase GitHub’s hosting logs through this site.

3. Contacting us by email

If you email us, the operator receives your sender address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. We use them to review your enquiry, reply, and resolve the related issue. Mail is received through Gmail, and processing by email providers also applies. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not publish your enquiry. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, sign-in tokens, payment details, or unnecessary personal information. You can request correction or deletion of correspondence at the same address. A single fixed retention period has not yet been established; the scope of deletion and any necessary retention are reviewed and explained for individual requests.

4. Data handling in the game under development

This is an explanation of the current implementation, not a statement that a live public service is available. Enabled features can differ between test builds.

  • Device saves: There are paths for storing progress, a mender’s name, story read status, and preferences on the device.
  • Accounts and backups: We are developing features that handle verified Google or Apple sign-in identifiers, an internal account ID, sessions, selected backups, and a server journey. The account is not identified by email alone, and we do not directly ask for your provider password.
  • Purchases and ad rewards: There is code to process store purchase evidence, account binding, and ad reward verification records. Live activation and the final provider setup are still pending. This website has no purchasing or advertising feature.
  • Deletion: Beyond deleting server journeys and backups, minimal purchase bindings, account and session metadata, and pending Apple authorization revocation data may remain. The deletion guide explains the current scope.

Before distributing the app, an app-specific policy must be finalized and published covering actual data, purposes, providers, transfers and storage locations, retention, deletion, advertising, age and consent handling, and user rights. This prelaunch notice is not a completed app policy or a promise that all data is erased immediately.

5. Changes to this notice

We will update this page and its date when the website or service’s data handling changes. The date shown on this page identifies the current notice.
