Privacy notice

This notice covers this website and email inquiries. A separate app privacy policy reflecting the actual service will be published before the game launches publicly.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

In development · Public downloads and accounts for general users are not yet available.

1. Operator and contact

The operator is Kim Hyeonwoo (김현우). Questions and requests to access, correct or delete information: ialskdji@gmail.com.

2. Website visits

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. The operator has added no analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, browser storage, contact forms or external fonts. Language links navigate to another page without saving a preference.

GitHub states that it logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. The operator cannot directly delete GitHub’s logs through this site.

3. Email inquiries

Sending an email provides your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to include. They are used to review and answer the inquiry and resolve the related issue. Email is received through Gmail; the Google Privacy Policy also applies. Inquiry content is not published.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, login tokens, payment details or unnecessary personal information. You can ask for email information to be corrected or deleted at the same address. There is currently no fixed retention period covering every inquiry. We review the information actually held and any need to retain it, then explain the scope and outcome of the request.

4. The game app and changes

Visiting this site does not provide us with game saves, login details or health data. Development and test app features and results depend on the version and connected services. This notice does not establish that production accounts, backups, deletion, purchases, ads or Watch features are operating.

Before the game’s public launch, an app policy will describe the actual data, purposes, providers, retention, deletion scope and user rights. This notice and its update date will change when website or email processing changes.
