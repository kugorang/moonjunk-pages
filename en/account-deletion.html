Account deletion

This page explains how to ask about deletion. It does not delete an account automatically.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

In development · Public downloads and accounts for general users are not yet available.

1. Current availability

Accounts for general users are not yet available. Deletion features in a test app depend on its version and actual service connection. Selecting a button, a network error or sending an email does not establish completed deletion.

2. Asking about deletion

Email ialskdji@gmail.com with the subject “Moonjunk account deletion request”. Initially, include only the sign-in method used (Google or Apple), the test app version if known, and why you cannot complete the request in the app.

A sender address alone does not establish account ownership or trigger immediate deletion. We will explain the information actually held, how ownership can be checked, the scope of deletion and retention, and the outcome. Do not send passwords, verification codes, tokens, card details, identity documents, original receipts or complete saves.

3. Different requests

Signing out or uninstalling an app does not replace a server account deletion request. Uninstalling may lose device saves. An account deletion request does not automatically issue a store refund.

The final scope and timing of deletion from operational backups and retained records will be explained in the app policy before public launch. This page does not promise immediate removal of all server information, device files or OS health records. Deletion or correction of email inquiry information can be requested separately.
